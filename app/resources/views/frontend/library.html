About the Library

A Legacy of
Knowledge & Access

University of Computer Studies (Taungoo) Library launched as soon as the university was established in 2007. It was then situated on the 1st floor of building No. D, with 1,300 volumes of books.

Digital software has been used to record library collections since 2013, supported by the Ministry of Science and Technology (MOST).

eCatalogues are provided at the website. Users can search by author, title, subject, keyword, and advanced search. At present, the library has nearly two hundred thousand collections — academic, reference, and fiction.
